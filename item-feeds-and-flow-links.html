5. Reading it as a flow

Flow Links are the edges of a graph: a Channel's output feeds another Channel's input, with a delay and an optional filter. This is Luc's own dock → sort → storage example.

Every Flow Link out of "Sorting" carries a filter (here, GTIN) and its own delay range; exactly one edge per source can be the catch-all "else" so nothing gets dropped on the floor.
